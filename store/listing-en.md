## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
Half the food you throw away was fine last week. You just forgot it was there.

Basil keeps track of what is in your fridge, freezer and cupboard, and tells you what to eat first — before it goes off.

SCAN, DON'T TYPE
Point the camera at a barcode and Basil fills in the product name. Point it at the printed date — "BEST BEFORE", "EXP", "USE BY" — and Basil reads the date for you. Both happen on your phone, in a couple of seconds. You just check it and save.

NUDGED, NOT NAGGED
Basil reminds you three days, two days and one day before something expires. Items due today or tomorrow arrive as a proper alert. The rest wait quietly in your notifications until you have time.

WHAT TO EAT FIRST
Your list is always sorted by urgency, not by when you added things. Eat today, this week, later. One tap marks something eaten or thrown away, and that is all the bookkeeping there is.

SEE YOUR WASTE SHRINK
Every month Basil shows how much you actually ate versus how much went in the bin, counted portion by portion. Not to make you feel guilty — just so you can watch the number go the right way.

ON YOUR HOME SCREEN
A free widget shows what is about to go off, without opening the app.

BUILT TO BE PRIVATE
No account. No sign-up. No servers of ours. Everything you add stays on your phone, and you can export the lot to a file whenever you want. There are no ads and no analytics, and nothing about you is sold to anyone.

FREE, WITH AN OPTIONAL UPGRADE
The free version keeps 25 items and four months of history, with every feature unlocked — scanning, reminders, the widget, the statistics.

Basil Pro removes the item limit and opens twelve months of history. Pay yearly or once for life. It is what keeps this app going without ads.

WORKS IN
English, Deutsch, Français, Tiếng Việt — including dates printed in the local format on packaging.

Basil is made by one person. If something is wrong or missing, write to dp7lab.support@gmail.com and a human will answer.
```

About 1,950 characters, well inside the limit.
