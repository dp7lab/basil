Scan the label, not your patience

Point the camera at a barcode to get the name, or at the printed date to get the expiry. Basil reads both on your phone, in a couple of seconds, and fills the form. You just check and save.

3·2·1

Nudged, not nagged

A reminder three, two and one day before. The urgent ones buzz. The rest wait quietly in your notifications. A home‑screen widget shows what's next to go.

%

Watch your waste shrink

Every month Basil shows what you ate and what you threw away, portion by portion. Not to make you feel bad. Just so you can see the number go down.

Private by design

No account, no servers, no analytics. Everything you add stays on your phone. The only thing that ever leaves is a barcode number, sent to Open Food Facts to look up a product name.
